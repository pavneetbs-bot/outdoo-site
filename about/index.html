About OUTDOO

Last updated 7 October 2026

OUTDOO is an online marketplace for outdoor living. Balconies, terraces, gardens, farmhouses and cafés: everything to furnish them, in one place. Our tagline says it: Every Outdoor Possibility.

Direct from the makers

We work directly with manufacturers, so you buy from the people who build the product, without a chain of middlemen in between. Every seller on OUTDOO is GST-registered and verified by us before their products go live.

Curated by people who build outdoor spaces

OUTDOO is a LifeWall Group Company. LifeWall designs and builds luxury outdoor spaces across Delhi NCR and has completed 300+ projects: pergolas, terraces, water features and more. The same team picks what goes on OUTDOO, choosing pieces that hold up to Indian sun, rain and dust.

What you can expect

  • Free delivery to top metros, with cash on delivery.
  • 7-day returns. See Returns, Refunds & Cancellations.
  • Damage-free promise: report transit damage within 48 hours and we sort it out.

Sell on OUTDOO

Make outdoor furniture or decor? Apply at seller.outdoo.in.

Company

OUTDOO is operated by Sunshine Upgrades Private Limited, Delhi NCR, India. Questions? Write to orders@outdoo.in.
