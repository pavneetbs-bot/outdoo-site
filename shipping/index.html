Shipping & Delivery

Last updated 7 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. Each product page shows where it can be delivered: some pieces ship anywhere by courier, some only within the maker's city and nearby. Enter your pincode on the product page to check. If your pincode can't be served, we call you before dispatch and cancel the order at no cost to you.

Delivery charges

Each product has a delivery charge, worked out from its packed size and weight, which depends on how far you are from where it ships: the seller's own city and nearby, the same state, or the rest of India. Many products deliver free. The product page shows the total delivered to your city, for example “₹1,500 delivered to Mumbai” (₹1,000 product + ₹500 packaging & delivery), and the cart and checkout add it by your pincode. Charges include GST and appear on the seller's tax invoice. Coupons apply to the product price.

When it ships

Every product page shows its dispatch time (for example “Ships in 7–10 days”). Many pieces are finished to order by the maker, so this is the time to get it ready and hand it to the courier. After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3–7 days depending on your city. We share tracking details once it's on the way.

If an order has products from more than one seller, they may arrive in separate deliveries.

Paying

Pay securely online at checkout: UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallets, through Razorpay. Prices include GST; the GST invoice comes from the seller.

When it arrives

  • Check the outer packaging before signing. If it's badly damaged, note it on the delivery slip or refuse the delivery.
  • Please record a short unboxing video — it makes any damage claim quick (see Damage-free promise).
  • Assembly is by you. Fittings and a step-by-step guide ship with every piece that needs it.

Questions

Write to support@outrr.in with your order number (starts with OD).
